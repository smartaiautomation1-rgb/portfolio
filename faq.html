FAQ

Common questions, honest answers.

If your question isn't here, ask it on the discovery call. I'd rather give a direct answer than write 20 FAQs about edge cases.

Engagement

How quickly can you start?

Usually within 48 hours of the discovery call. I keep a tight pipeline so projects move fast — usually 1–2 active builds at a time.

Do you only work through Upwork?

Upwork is my preferred channel — verified reviews, escrow, and clear contracts. For larger engagements I can also work direct via a standard MSA + SOW.

Will I own the system you build?

Yes. All code, prompts, workflows, and documentation are handed off and yours forever. No proprietary hosting, no lock-in.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — a mutual NDA is standard before sharing anything sensitive. Happy to sign yours or use a clean one.

Technical

Can you work with our existing stack?

Almost always. I integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Intercom, Shopify, Stripe, Slack, and dozens more. If there's an API, I can talk to it.

Where does the data live?

In your account. n8n self-hosted on Railway/Render, vectors in your Pinecone or Supabase, LLM calls go through your OpenAI/Anthropic keys. I don't proxy your data.

What about model costs?

I estimate them upfront and tune for cost in the build. Most chatbots run $20–200/mo in LLM spend. Voice agents are usage-based — typically $0.08–0.15 per minute.

Is the work GDPR / SOC2 compliant?

I build to be compliant with whatever framework you're under. EU-region inference, data residency, audit logging — all doable. For SOC2/HIPAA we'll discuss the specifics on the call.

Pricing

Fixed price or hourly?

Almost always fixed-price, scoped tightly after the audit. You know the cost and the deliverable before we start. Hourly only for open-ended R&D engagements.

What does a typical project cost?

AI automations start at $1,200. Chatbots at $1,800. Voice agents at $2,400. Marketing automation at $1,500. Larger or multi-system builds scope individually.

Do you offer retainers?

Yes — optional monthly retainer for monitoring, model upgrades, and new automations. Typically $1.5K–4K/mo depending on system count and uptime expectations.

What's the refund policy?

If we hit a milestone and the work doesn't meet the agreed acceptance criteria, I fix it on my time. If a build is truly unsalvageable (rare), Upwork dispute resolution handles it cleanly.

Still have questions?

Ask them on the discovery call.
