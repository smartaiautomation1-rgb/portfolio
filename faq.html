FAQ

Common questions, honest answers.

If your question isn't here, ask it on the discovery call. I'd rather give a direct answer than write 20 FAQs about edge cases.

Engagement

How quickly can you start?

Usually within 48 hours of the discovery call. I keep a tight pipeline — 1–2 active builds at a time.

Do you only work through Upwork?

Upwork is preferred — verified reviews, escrow, clear contracts. For larger engagements I also work direct via a standard MSA + SOW.

Will I own the system you build?

Yes. All code, prompts, workflows, and documentation are handed off and yours forever. No proprietary hosting, no lock-in.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — a mutual NDA is standard before sharing anything sensitive. Happy to sign yours or use a clean one.

Technical

Can you work with our existing stack?

Almost always. I integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Intercom, Shopify, Stripe, Slack, and dozens more. If there's an API, I can talk to it.

Where does the data live?

In your account. n8n self-hosted, vectors in your Pinecone or Supabase, LLM calls through your own keys. I don't proxy your data.

What about model costs?

Estimated upfront and tuned for cost in the build. Most chatbots run $20–200/mo. Voice agents are usage-based — typically $0.08–0.15 per minute.

Is the work GDPR / SOC2 compliant?

I build to whatever framework you're under. EU-region inference, data residency, audit logging — all doable. For SOC2/HIPAA we'll discuss specifics on the call.

Pricing

Fixed price or hourly?

Almost always fixed-price, scoped after the audit. You know cost and deliverable before we start. Hourly only for open-ended R&D.

What does a typical project cost?

Automations from $1,200. Chatbots $1,800. Voice agents $2,400. Marketing automation $1,500. Larger or multi-system builds scope individually.

Do you offer retainers?

Yes — optional monthly retainer for monitoring, model upgrades, and new automations. Typically $1.5K–4K/mo depending on scope.

What's the refund policy?

If a milestone doesn't meet the agreed acceptance criteria, I fix it on my time. For unsalvageable builds (rare), Upwork dispute resolution handles it cleanly.

Still have questions?

Ask them on the discovery call.

30 minutes. Free. No deck. We just talk.
