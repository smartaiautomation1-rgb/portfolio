Why this stack

Pragmatic, not religious.

I have opinions, not allegiances. The right tool depends on volume, team skill, budget, and what's already in your stack.

  • Self-hosted by default

    When data sensitivity matters. n8n on Railway/Render keeps your data in your account.

  • Frontier when it earns it

    GPT-4o or Claude for high-stakes reasoning. Smaller models for the 80% of tasks that don't need a Ferrari.

  • No-code → code escape hatch

    Start fast in n8n. If we hit the ceiling, I rewrite the hot path in Node or Python.

  • Observability from day one

    Logs, traces, cost dashboards. You should know what every dollar of LLM spend bought you.
