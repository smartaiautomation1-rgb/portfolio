Process

From idea to live system in 7 days.

Four phases. No mystery, no consultant-speak. Here's exactly what happens when we work together — including what's not included.

01
Day 0 · 30 min

Discovery

A focused 30-minute call. I listen to where work piles up, ask sharp questions to find the actual bottleneck (not the symptom), and map the data flow.

You leave with

  • A clear yes/no on whether AI is the right lever right now
  • A rough ROI estimate (hours saved / revenue unlocked)
  • Honest opinion — even if that means "don't hire me"
02
Day 1–2 · 48 hrs

Blueprint

A scoped plan within 48 hours of the call. Fixed price, fixed timeline, deliverables enumerated. You see exactly what you're paying for before any code is written.

You leave with

  • Notion doc with architecture diagram + data flow
  • Itemized deliverables + acceptance criteria
  • Fixed price + day-by-day timeline
03
Day 3–10

Build

Working v1 in 5–10 days. Daily two-minute Loom updates so you always know where we are. No "I'll have something to show you next week" energy.

You leave with

  • Daily Loom updates (2–3 min each)
  • Staging environment to test before flipping the switch
  • Direct Slack/WhatsApp line during build hours
04
Day 11–14

Launch & iterate

We flip the switch together. I monitor for 14 days post-launch — free tuning, prompt tweaks, edge-case fixes. Then full handoff with documentation and training.

You leave with

  • Live system + 14 days of post-launch tuning
  • Notion runbook for your team (edit + extend without me)
  • 30-min training Loom + Q&A session
  • Optional monthly retainer for ongoing improvements

Honest disclosure

What's not included.

Most "AI consultants" hide the gaps until you've already signed. Here's the truth upfront.

  • Long discovery decks

    I won't sell you a 6-week discovery phase. If it takes that long to find the problem, the problem isn't AI.

  • Proprietary lock-in

    No "kenniz cloud" you can't escape. Everything I build runs on tools you can hire someone else for.

  • SLA-style hand-holding

    I'm a freelancer, not a managed service. The 14-day post-launch window is generous; after that, retainer or ad-hoc.

  • Scope creep without conversation

    If the project doubles in size mid-build, we re-scope and re-price. Transparent, always.
